## Prerequisites

- **Required:** none formally, but the course assumes basic familiarity with probability and random variables (e.g. from STA100)
- **Recommended:** Mathematical Methods 1 (MAT100), Mathematical Methods 2 (MAT200), Probability and Statistics 1 (STA100)
- Get comfortable with **RStudio and R** before the semester starts

## Tools

- **R / RStudio** — all exercises and simulations
- Textbook: *Statistical Computing with R*, 2nd ed., Maria L. Rizzo (CRC Press) — R code for examples: https://github.com/mariarizzo/SCR2e
- Canvas — weekly pages, assignments, announcements

## Assessment structure

**Coursework requirement (gate to the exam)**
- **2 mandatory assignments**, graded pass/fail — both must pass to be allowed to sit the exam
- Due in week 39 (assignment 1) and week 42 (assignment 2)
- May be completed in English or Norwegian
- AI tools may be used to clarify concepts or check your own finished solutions, but not to solve the exercises for you

**Written exam**
- 4 hours, pen & paper, on campus
- Questions in English; you may answer in English or Norwegian
- Allowed aids: a **standard (non-programmable) calculator**, the course's `tablesformulas.pdf` (provided with the exam), and **one A4 sheet of your own handwritten notes** (must be handwritten directly on the sheet — printed text not allowed)
- ⚠️ Canvas lists the exam date as **November 20, 2025** — this looks like leftover text from a previous year's page (today is Sep 2026, in the 2026–2027 academic year). Confirm the actual autumn 2026 exam date on Canvas/StudentWeb rather than trusting this figure.
