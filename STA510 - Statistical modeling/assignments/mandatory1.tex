\section{Problem 1}\label{problem-1}

\subsection{a)}\label{a}

\(f(x;a)\) is non-negative and integrate to one, therefore is a valid
PDF.

For \(a \leq x \leq a+20\), both factors are non-negative:

\[
x-a \geq 0,
\qquad
a+20-x \geq 0.
\]

Since \(c > 0\), it follows that

\[
f(x;a) \geq 0.
\]

To find the normalising constant, we require

\[
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(x;a)\,dx = 1.
\]

Since the density is zero outside \([a,a+20]\),

\[
\int_a^{a+20} c(x-a)(a+20-x)\,dx = 1.
\]

Using the substitution

\[
u = x-a,
\qquad
dx = du,
\]

the limits become

\[
x=a \Rightarrow u=0,
\qquad
x=a+20 \Rightarrow u=20.
\]

Also,

\[
a+20-x = a+20-(u+a)=20-u.
\]

Therefore,

\[
1 = c\int_0^{20}u(20-u)\,du.
\]

\[
1 = c\int_0^{20}(20u-u^2)\,du.
\]

\[
1 = c\left[10u^2-\frac{u^3}{3}\right]_0^{20}.
\]

\[
1 = c\left(10\cdot20^2-\frac{20^3}{3}\right).
\]

\[
1 = c\left(4000-\frac{8000}{3}\right)
  = c\frac{4000}{3}.
\]

Hence,

\[
\boxed{c=\frac{3}{4000}}.
\]

Thus, the valid pdf is

\[
f(x;a)=
\begin{cases}
\displaystyle \frac{3}{4000}(x-a)(a+20-x),
& a \leq x \leq a+20,\\[6pt]
0, & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]
